BOKRANA.

Overview

BOKRANA gives Arabic-speaking beginners a route from a practical question to a project they can share. The public course and private student dashboard connect the lessons, enrollment and final project.

The problem

A list of AI tools gives a learner little guidance on how to use them in a real assignment. The course uses practical work and instructor feedback to connect individual sessions.

Who it serves

Arabic-speaking students, teachers and professionals learning to use generative AI in their work.

My contribution

I teach the generative AI course and review submitted projects. The curriculum covers research, content creation, presentations and website building.

System design

The platform explains enrollment, takes learners through a four-week course and accepts their capstone through the student dashboard. Learners can opt to submit work for the public showcase.

Tools & components

  • Arabic and English interface
  • Student dashboard
  • Project review

Results & trade-offs

A live four-week course with eight scheduled sessions and a capstone submission path.

Implementation decisions

Reviewing payment and project submissions adds a manual step. It gives the instructor control over access and which work is made public.

Testing & reliability

Course access begins after payment approval. Public examples are labeled separately from student work, and the showcase describes the instructor review required before publication.

Data handling

The syllabus and course overview are public. Enrollment and payment review involve student data, so the walkthrough shows the public learner experience.

Media & walkthroughs

Next steps

I would publish an opt-in capstone walkthrough that documents the learner's question, tool choices, revisions and final result.
